# Welcome to Bloxburg Horror!

You and up to three other investigators build your decks and work together to solve the ancient mysteries of the world, battling cultists, monsters, and elder gods along the way.

Each **campaign** is a self-contained story told across multiple **scenarios**, with many branching paths and choices that affect the outcome of the whole campaign. Scenarios are where the actual gameplay takes place.

How you build your deck defines who you are. You can be a slippery operator who solves objectives while avoiding enemies, a medic that keeps the team alive and well, a bouncer taking the fight to the monsters, a dark mage casting game-altering spells, or something else totally unique, and entirely your own.

However, you must keep in mind: each character has their own weakness, one that comes to haunt them at a time most inconvenient: An old enemy; a chronic illness; visions of dark worlds; or perhaps something even worse. Teamwork and good planning are key to making it out alive.

## Before You Play

There is no in-game tutorial, so it's best to familiarize yourself with the [full rules](https://images-cdn.fantasyflightgames.com/filer_public/dd/78/dd7818fe-0c9a-4a6c-b685-e32ab55b1702/ahc60_learn_to_play_web.pdf) before asking questions such as *"Why did I take so much damage after drawing a card?"*

Have fun!
